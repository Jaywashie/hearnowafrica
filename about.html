About HearNow Africa

We believe every person deserves the chance to hear the people they love.

Our Mission

HearNow Africa exists to make hearing healthcare reachable for children and adults across the continent who could not otherwise afford it. We connect families with audiologists, hearing devices, and the follow-up care needed to make a lasting difference.

Untreated hearing loss can hold a child back in school and cut an adult off from work and community. We work to change that outcome, one person at a time.

Our Story

HearNow Africa began with a small group of audiologists and volunteers who saw how many people in their communities were living with hearing loss that had never been diagnosed, let alone treated. What started as a handful of weekend screening clinics has grown into a network of partner clinics and volunteers working across several countries.

What Guides Us

Dignity first

Every person we support is treated with respect, patience, and care, not as a case number.

Local partnership

We work through local clinics and community volunteers who understand the communities we serve.

Long-term care

A hearing device is just the start. We stay involved for fittings, adjustments, and follow-up visits.

Transparency

Donors and partners can always see how support reaches the people who need it.
