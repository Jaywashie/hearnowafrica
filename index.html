Bringing sound to life across Africa.

HearNow Africa helps children and adults with hearing loss get the hearing healthcare, devices, and support they need to learn, work, and connect with the people around them.

Apply for Support Learn About Us

How we help

We work with local clinics, audiologists, and community volunteers to make hearing care reachable for families who need it most.

♪

Hearing Devices & Care

We help cover the cost of hearing aids, cochlear implants, and follow-up care for children and adults who could not otherwise afford them.

👥

Community Support

Families get guidance, sign language resources, and connection to a community of people who understand what they're going through.

📚

Awareness & Screening

We run local hearing screening days and awareness campaigns to catch hearing loss early, especially in children.
